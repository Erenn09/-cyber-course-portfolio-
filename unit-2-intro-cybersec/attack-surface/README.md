### 3. Reflection

I noticed that my biggest weakness is that MFA is not enabled on all of my important accounts. This week, I am going to activate the MFA on my most important accounts where this feature has not been enabled. It has become evident that protection of one account helps in the safeguarding of several other aspects of my digital life too.
