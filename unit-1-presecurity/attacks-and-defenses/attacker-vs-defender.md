# Attacker vs Defender — Personal Email Account

## The system

I chose my personal email account because I use it almost every day. I also use it for logging into other websites and services, so losing my email account could cause problems with other accounts too.

## Attacker view

An attacker could send me a fake email with a link that looks like a normal login page. If I enter my email and password there, the attacker could get my login details. They could then log into my email, read my private emails and try to reset passwords for my other accounts.

## Defender view

I would use multi-factor authentication  on my email account. This means that even if someone gets my password, they would still need another verification step to log in. Because of this, the attacker would have a harder time getting into my account. However, multi-factor authentication cannot protect me from everything, for example if my device already has malware or if I approve a fake login by mistake.

## CIA angle

The main threat is Confidentiality because an attacker could read my private emails and account information. Integrity could also be affected if they changed my settings or sent emails from my account.

## What I'm changing this week

This week I will check if multi-factor authentication is enabled on my email account. I will also check my security and recovery settings and make sure they are up to date.
